# CHET GTA

*A Qin Academy story · v3 (I-005), matches `storyline.md` v3*

---

Snow is melting on the mat of a downtown Montreal grocery store. Everything looks like GTA VI: glossy, saturated, a little too real. **Chet**, Qin Academy's tutor, stops in front of a wall of blue boxes. He picks up a box of **KD**, turns it over and studies it like he's choosing a weapon. Then he drops it into his basket.

In a small circle at the bottom of the screen we see **Sobhi** in real life. He's the one holding the controller and playing Chet. He nods at his screen.

> **SOBHI:** This makes sense in this economy.

Chet's phone buzzes. Then again, and again.

> *"do u have a final crash course for ENGR 213??"*
> *"i need to study for MATH 205 😭"*
> *"my CHEM 205 final is in 2 weeks"*
> *"is there a final recap for PHYS 205?"*

The messages stack up. Chet's calm face slowly turns into worry as he walks down the aisle. In the bubble, Sobhi winces.

At the end of the aisle, a **student** steps out and lights up when she sees him.

> **STUDENT:** Hi Chet! Thank you for the review for midterm 2, I actually did pretty good! My finals are coming up… is there gonna be a final crash course?

Chet answers a beat too late.

> **CHET:** Yes. We're working on something.

She waves and leaves. Sobhi smirks. Chet heads to the checkout.

---

The **cashier** doesn't even look up.

> **CASHIER:** Bonjour-hi! Is that all for today?

Chet puts the KD on the belt. It slides toward the scanner.

> **CASHIER:** Cash or card?

*Beep.*

Everything slows down. The store turns purple and teal and goes soft at the edges. The music cuts out, leaving only a low hum and a heartbeat. In the bubble, Sobhi freezes in grey, as if time has stopped for him too.

**TRANS—ACTION**

The cashier's lips are still moving, but the voice coming out isn't hers anymore. It's the student's.

> **CASHIER (in the student's voice):** When will the final crash course be?

The texts come back as whispers: *ENGR 213… MATH 205… two weeks…* Course codes float through the air like store signs. The camera pushes slowly into Chet's eyes.

*Snap.*

Colour, sound and speed come back all at once. The cashier is leaning toward him.

> **CASHIER:** Sir… are you here?

Chet blinks.

> **CASHIER:** I said, cash or card?

He taps his card. *Beep.* Sobhi is trying not to laugh.

---

Outside, light snow is falling on the parking lot. Chet walks to his car, gets in and pulls out his phone.

*Calling SOBHI…*

In the bubble, a real phone buzzes on Sobhi's desk next to his controller. He looks at the game, then at his phone. The circle grows until it fills the bottom half of the screen: the game on top, real life below.

Sobhi answers.

> **CHET:** Sobhi. Can we finalize?

A pause.

> **SOBHI:** …It will be announced soon.
