# Chet GTA — storyline v2 (I-003)

v1 (I-001) was the user's plan plus proposed additions. v2 applies the answers from I-003: no stars, Sobhi has only two reaction lines, the rush stays, the bubble grows to half the screen, the world is Montreal, there are French touches, Sobhi's new final line, the real KD box, and no loop. Lines in quotes are a draft until approved; they will be tuned to Chet's and Sobhi's real way of talking after the @qinacademy reel study.

**One line:** Finals season turns a Montreal grocery run into a mission for Qin Academy's Chet, and the player behind the game is the one who will announce the crash course.
**Feeling at the end:** "Ha, that's so us." Then: "I need that crash course."
