Got questions?

What is the AI Editor?

A chat-to-edit planner. You describe the edit, it plans cuts, captions, motion, B-roll, music and sound on a six-track timeline and explains each decision. You adjust anything by hand, then copy the brief into Claude Code to render it.

Does my footage get uploaded?

No. The clip plays in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it render the video in the browser?

Not yet. The browser plans the edit; the render happens on your machine with Remotion and the free skills, so you keep full quality and editable layers.

Do I need paid tools?

No. Every paid service on the skill stack lists a free local route: OmniVoice or Piper for voice, Whisper for transcripts, code-made or open-source music, ComfyUI for footage.

Where do the styles come from?

From public creator reels studied frame by frame. Techniques are copied; footage, voices, text and branding never are.

Does it make deepfakes or fake speakers?

No. It edits your real footage. Voice cloning and character swaps stay off unless the person has agreed to it.

Which formats does it support?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, square 1:1 and widescreen 16:9.
