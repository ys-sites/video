# Platform artwork

These local marks identify export destinations and the platforms where referenced posts were shared. They do not imply endorsement or a connected publishing account. Brand names and trademarks belong to their owners. Asset fills preserve platform identity and are not interface theme colors.

- TikTok/Douyin musical-note marks and Instagram artwork: [Iconify Logos](https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets/tree/master/json), `@iconify-json/logos` 1.2.20, CC0-1.0. Vectors are preserved, wrapped in their original viewBox. Douyin and TikTok share the musical-note mark.
- Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube Shorts and X: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.38.0), 16.38.0, CC0-1.0. Vectors are preserved, with brand-color fills and decorative titles removed. See [CC0 license](CC0-LICENSE.txt).
- WeChat Channels: [Remix Icon](https://github.com/Remix-Design/RemixIcon/tree/v4.9.1), 4.9.1, `wechat-channels-fill`, Remix Icon License v1.0. Vector is preserved with the Channels orange fill. See [license](remix-icon-LICENSE.txt).

[sources.json](sources.json) records each mapping and source version. No runtime icon CDN or additional npm dependency is used.
