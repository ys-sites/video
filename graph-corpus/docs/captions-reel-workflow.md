# Speech-timed captions reel

Source: the user-provided text and screenshots from “How I edited my captions reel with AI.” This describes the demonstrated approach, not a verified result from our tools. The reference shows an optional AI-replaced room, a person mask, word timing against a waveform, text on both sides of the subject, sound timed to motion, and successive frame-specific revisions.

## Production sequence

1. Review the actual footage and, when supplied, three to five reference reels. Record specific choices for typography, scale, placement, movement, color, timing, depth, grading and sound. Separate observed examples from the user's approved preferences.
2. Make a rough speech edit and retain the original voice. Transcribe locally if an available tool supports it, correct the transcript by listening, and check word onsets against the waveform and playback. Automatic word timings are provisional; do not call them frame-accurate until checked.
3. Create editable text layers with a readable base typeface and a contrasting emphasis face. Use the accent color purposefully; allow phrases enough time to read. Keep important captions away from likely bottom and right-side platform overlays, then inspect on the actual target platform and frame size.
4. For depth, composite background, text behind the speaker, the subject matte and foreground text. Check the matte for moving hair, hands, occlusion and flicker. If a clean matte is unavailable, keep the text in front or use another supported treatment. Use subtle camera motion only when it serves the shot.
5. An AI room replacement, such as the service depicted in the reference, is optional and requires a genuinely available integration and appropriate permission for the media and provider. Retain the original; do not claim a replacement or relight occurred without producing and reviewing it.
6. When sound design is requested, place appropriate licensed ticks, impacts or whooshes on the relevant visual events and mix beneath speech. Prioritize intelligibility; silence or fewer effects may serve the edit better. Verify by listening to the actual mix.
7. Export a review preview. Record notes against the exact video version and time or frame, including the requested adjustment. Apply the notes, verify the changed moments, and save explicitly approved style choices in that project's taste file. Do not treat the reference creator's five rounds or sound count as fixed requirements.

Use the adjacent `caption-review-notes.template.json` as a starting point for a particular edit. Keep original media, transcript, notes and versions separate; this workflow does not itself transcribe, matte, call an AI service or render a video.
