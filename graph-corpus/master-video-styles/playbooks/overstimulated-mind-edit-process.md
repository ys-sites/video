# Overstimulated Mind — Edit Process (6 steps, 7 days)

Source: `Overstimulated_Mind_Edit_Process.pdf` (uploaded Nov 17, 2026).
Subject: Veronika talking head — laziness / discipline / phone / overstimulated mind.
This is the detailed build process behind the Sumit-style edit (Style 1 in
`sumit-kelsie-edit-styles.md`).

## Step 1 — Hook Strategy & Setup (Day 0–1)

- Analyze the raw talking-head footage to craft the opening hook
  ("There is a version OF THIS VIDEO").
- Set up the project timeline, frame rate, sync the original speaking audio.
- Identify key emphasis words in the script (e.g. "Lazy", "undisciplined", "PHONE").
- Establish the core visual theme: Orange/Brown glow, scattered typography, stark
  grayscale background shifts.
- Tools: Premiere Pro / Notion / Google Docs. Deliverable: synced timeline & edit plan.

## Step 2 — Masking & Roto Brush (Day 1–2)

- Isolate the speaker from the original background with advanced Roto Brush.
- Apply the signature animated orange/brown neon outline around the exact silhouette.
- Keyframe background adjustments: natural room → stark white/grayscale for high contrast.
- Clean mask edges around hair and hands during fast movements.
- Tools: After Effects / Photoshop. Output: rotoscoped subject & dynamic backgrounds.

## Step 3 — B-Roll & PiP Collages (Day 2–3)

- Gather and trim secondary footage (driving car, bedroom lounging, walking outdoors)
  for multi-cam sequences.
- Design Picture-in-Picture collages: several clips on screen at once, drop shadows.
- Extract and prep 3D visual assets, e.g. the dartboard icon for the "discipline" cue.
- Keep layout compositions clear behind the main subject.
- Tools: Premiere Pro / After Effects. Output: pre-composed collage scenes & assets.

## Step 4 — Kinetic Typography (Day 3–5)

- Animate bold text popping up word-by-word (e.g. "Lazy", "undisciplined", "PHONE"),
  synced perfectly to the speaker's pacing.
- Custom scattered text effect for the phrase "and push through things" — words placed
  randomly across the screen.
- Varied fonts: serif for accents ("Lazy"), clean sans-serif for main subtitles.
  Alternate between orange and white.
- Scale bounces and rotational keyframes for energy.
- Tools: After Effects. Output: animated draft v1 (text & motion synced).

## Step 5 — SFX & Color Polish (Day 5–6)

- Precise SFX: whooshes for collage slide-ins, distinct pops/clicks for every kinetic
  word reveal.
- Balance voiceover EQ with the sound design — SFX never overpower speech.
- Warm color grade, enhancing the orange/brown aesthetic while keeping natural skin tones.
- Review grayscale background transitions for smooth execution.
- Tools: Audition / After Effects / Premiere Pro. Output: polished cut v2.

## Step 6 — Review & Final Export (Day 6–7)

- Final playback review: rapid text animations and PiP collages perfectly timed.
- Apply client revisions (e.g. tweak scattered text positions, SFX volume).
- Export final video in high quality (H.264 MP4), optimized for social media.
- Organize final project files, render a backup to secure cloud storage.
- Tools: Media Encoder / Frame.io / Google Drive. Output: final motion graphics video.
