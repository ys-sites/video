# The Ariya Store Tour — Satellite to Showroom

Style reference: https://www.instagram.com/p/C9QFdpTg2nO/
Marché Ariya (@marcheariya, verified) — Supermarché / BBQ au Charbon, 3207 Boul.
Taschereau, Greenfield Park and 559 Chem. St-Jean, La Prairie, QC. 30–40 second
vertical store tour, posted Jul 10, 2024, 1,521 likes. No presenter, no voiceover,
no interview. Watched in full in the live browser Nov 18, 2026 (three loops).

## The style in one line

A calm, polished showroom tour: a Google Earth-style satellite zoom drops you onto
the store, then the camera glides through about ten departments in slow 2–4 second
shots while one continuous music bed plays. The space sells itself.

## Format

- Vertical portrait 9:16, about 30–40 seconds (no timecode on the player; timed from three loop cycles).
- One continuous store-tour commercial. No presenter, no people speaking on camera.

## Hook — first 8 seconds (longest single shot)

- Opens on a very high, Google Earth-style satellite/aerial view of a residential
  street grid, then zooms/descends continuously and smoothly toward the store,
  passing through a thin layer of cloud/fog.
- Mid-zoom, the only added text overlay appears: "3207 Bd Taschereau, Greenfield
  Park" in bold white sans-serif, centred, inside a thin white rectangular
  border/frame that draws in around it. It holds for a few seconds while the zoom
  continues, then disappears.
- The zoom lands as a low, oblique drone shot of the storefront — red "ARIYA" sign,
  red awning, turquoise sky, parking lot with yellow lines and bollards — then
  settles into a straight-on front view of the "ARIYA SUPERMARCHÉ" entrance for
  about 2–3 seconds. No other hook text, no countdown, no face-to-camera.

## Pacing and cut rhythm

- Slow opening, then a steady interior montage: shots last roughly 2–4 seconds each,
  with regular hard cuts. Not frenetic — no jump cuts, freeze frames, speed ramps or
  split screens.
- Within almost every shot the camera is still moving slowly and smoothly: a slow
  push-in, a gentle pan, or a forward tracking move down an aisle (gimbal/drone-smooth,
  no handheld shake). The cut lands while the previous shot is still gliding, which
  gives the whole tour a calm, floating feel.

## Visuals per beat — interior tour (about 8 s to about 30 s)

1. Kabab ARIYA dining area: wooden tables and chairs, a window with reversed lettering ("Authenticité & saveurs…") and an Ariya logo.
2. Meat/deli fridge: trays of sausages, steaks and packaged meats, with price tags.
3. Produce aisle, low angle: lemons and melons on the left, a central wooden display, a "Viande / Poisson" sign in the far background.
4. Produce/deli wall under a "Fraîcheur de la nature" sign: packaged produce on top shelves, green crates of lemons, limes and oranges, leeks below.
5. Overhead close-up of bulk bins: pistachios, black raisins and cashews, with metal scoops and a price tag reading "11.39$" (upside down in frame).
6. Spice aisle: shelves of jars and bottles with red, green and white labels, slow pan.
7. Kabab ARIYA counter, close: menu screens, "Kabab ARIYA" and "commandez ici" signage, blue decorative tile border, black marble counter, food trays.
8. Oil/sauce aisle: bottles on a wooden display, a "Produits laitiers" sign in the background.
9. Bakery display case: stacked cookies/biscuits on glass shelves with blue LED strip lighting.
10. Tagines: a golden conical clay tagine on a shelf, with other tagines and boxes above it.
11. Wide centre-aisle tracking shot: shelves on the left, a deli case on the right, camera moving forward down the aisle; a similar wide bulk-aisle shot (wooden bulk bins left, shelves right, "Viande / Poisson" in the back) also appears.

Shot order in the middle of the tour is as observed most consistently across three
passes, not frame-by-frame certain — a couple of adjacent 2-second shots may sit in a
different order.

## Closing (final seconds)

- A hero branding shot: a close-up of the countertop ARIYA sign — circular
  yellow/red logo with a green motif and "ARIYA®" in red below it, reflected on a
  glass counter, with bakery sweets in the foreground and a shopper in the background.
- Then a dark, dimmed exterior storefront shot that fades/dims toward black, and the
  video loops straight back to the high satellite view. No written call-to-action,
  no "follow us", no hours, phone number or website on screen at the end.

## On-screen text, captions and watermark

- Added text: only the address overlay. No lower thirds, no price graphics, no
  subtitles/closed captions and no end-card text.
- The French words visible in the video ("Fraîcheur de la nature", "Viande / Poisson",
  "Produits laitiers", "Kabab ARIYA", "commandez ici") are physical in-store signage,
  not added overlays.
- A small circular Ariya logo watermark sits at bottom-centre through most interior
  shots (and some exterior shots), semi-transparent and unobtrusive. It is absent from
  the opening satellite zoom.

## Transitions

- Mostly hard cuts between interior shots.
- Opening: a single continuous zoom/descend, not a cut.
- One fast radial/zoom-blur swoop over the bulk bins (yellow and dark, heavily motion-blurred) between shots.
- Once, a golden tagine filled the frame with a thin bright-blue outline/glow around
  its silhouette over an aisle background, then cut to the same tagine on its shelf —
  a brief highlight/transition effect lasting under a second.
- Final dim/fade to dark before the loop restarts.

## Sound / music

Continuous music throughout, with no spoken voiceover, dialogue or narration. No
track title, artist or genre is credited on the post page, and the audio could not
be identified — do not recreate it from a named song. Use any continuous, unobtrusive
instrumental music bed under the tour, with no voiceover.

## How to recreate it, step by step

1. Shoot vertical 9:16.
2. Open with a satellite-to-ground zoom (Google Earth-style) lasting about 8 seconds,
   fading in the white bordered address text in bold white sans-serif at about
   2–4 seconds and removing it before landing.
3. Land on a low oblique, then front-on, storefront shot.
4. Hard-cut through about 10–12 interior departments in 2–4-second shots, each with a
   slow push-in, pan or forward aisle track.
5. Keep the small circular logo watermark at bottom-centre through the interior.
6. Add one zoom-blur swoop and one brief outlined highlight on a hero product (tagine).
7. Close on a full-frame logo sign, then dim/fade on the exterior.
8. Lay a single continuous instrumental music track underneath, no voiceover, no
   subtitles, no end-card text.

## Feel

Bright, clean, polished and inviting — a smooth showroom tour, not a vlog or a sale
announcement. Daylight and warm interior light, saturated colour (turquoise sky, red
signage, colourful produce), smooth gliding camera, no people as presenters.
