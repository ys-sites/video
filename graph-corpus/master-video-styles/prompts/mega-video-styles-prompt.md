## How to use this prompt

1. Name the style (by name or number), or describe the footage and let Claude pick:
   explainer / documentary = Sumit; numbered tips = Kelsie listicle; house tour =
   Kelsie listing tour / fly-through; redevelopment pitch = Melissa vision pitch; shop
   or showroom tour = Ariya store tour; quick polish = Romina's 5 edits;
   motion-graphics video = MotionSmith; paper collage = InVideo paper-cutout.
2. Post the raw footage file, topic, target duration and platform.
3. Claude storyboards every beat first — keyword text, icon or number badge,
   animation — and shows the storyboard before rendering.
4. Claude builds the edit in After Effects (or the tool the style names) and hands
   back the video plus a one-line summary.

## STYLE 1 — The Sumit Style

Creator: Sumit (@sumit.visions). Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfRAkn4xIZy/
("Claude vs Human Creativity" — the HUMAN edit is the target; the Claude edit is what NOT to copy).

**Look:** speaker on camera the whole time; every spoken idea gets a big animated keyword plus a simple illustration of the metaphor; one colour through the whole video (pink in the reference); fast and clean.

**Style bible**
- Speaker stays on screen. Graphics support them, never replace them.
- One colour theme per video — backgrounds, text, icons, illustrations all in that palette.
- One visual per spoken beat: kinetic-text keyword + simple metaphor icon (brain = overthinking, shield = protection, compass/target = goals). No visual without a spoken reason.
- Fast, clean pacing. New visual on nearly every beat. No dead air.
- Real photos only when they prove something (diploma, book cover, result).
- Keywords big, bold, animated in exactly on the beat of the spoken word.
- Documentary tone: clear, confident, educational. Never cheesy, never cluttered.

**Build steps:** Hook & setup (Premiere, Notion, Docs) → Masking & roto with neon theme-colour outline, background keyframed to white/grayscale (After Effects, Photoshop) → B-roll & PiP collages with drop shadows, 3D metaphor assets → Kinetic typography (word-by-word pops, scattered text, serif accents + sans subtitles, alternate theme colour and white, scale bounces, rotation) → SFX & colour (whooshes on collage slide-ins, pop/click per word, SFX never over speech, warm grade in theme colour) → Review & export (H.264 MP4, back up).

**Copy-paste prompt:** Edit this footage in the Sumit style: the speaker stays on screen the whole time, one colour theme for the whole video, one visual per spoken beat (a kinetic-text keyword plus a simple icon of the metaphor), fast clean pacing with no dead air, keywords big and bold animated exactly on the beat.

Tools: After Effects, Premiere Pro, Audition, Photoshop, MotionSmith, Claude Code + After Effects MCP.

## STYLE 2 — The Kelsie Listicle

Creator: Kelsie Blevins (@_k.elsie), top 1% realtor, North Carolina. Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DewTzCzxR9q/
("Thinking about selling? Here's everything you need to know in under 30 seconds." 12 seller tips, seated talking head, visible countdown timer.)

**Style bible**
- Hook = promise + visible clock: "everything you need to know in under 30 seconds" with a countdown timer on screen the whole video.
- Numbered rapid-fire list: 12 tips in ~35 s, ~3 s per tip, a numbered badge counting up.
- Animated keyword per tip — short phrases only ("Don't overprice", "Marketing Plan", "Strategy"), never full sentences.
- Seated talking head, direct to camera, bright clean interior; confident, instructional.
- One idea per tip — if it needs two sentences it is two tips or it is cut.
- End card with two CTAs: "Save this for later" + contact (DM / call). The save prompt is the point.
- Caption carries the full numbered list so it works on mute and gets saved as a checklist.

**Copy-paste prompt:** Edit this footage in the Kelsie listicle style: a numbered rapid-fire list with a visible 30-second countdown timer on screen the whole time, about 3 seconds per tip, one big animated keyword phrase per tip (short phrases only, never full sentences), ending with a "Save this for later" card plus a contact call to action. Also write the full numbered-list caption.

## STYLE 3 — Kelsie Listing Tour — The Wilmore Format

Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfzaFE1xv02/ — 2232 Wilmore Dr, Charlotte, $649,950 all-brick bungalow, 3 bed / 2 bath / 1,504 sqft, 53 s (Nov 16, 2026).

**Build steps**
1. Hook on lifestyle first, not the house — walk the neighbourhood (market, coffee): "What if this? and this? minutes from your door."
2. Fly-through push-in zoom — exterior straight into the interior in one continuous move (drone/gimbal push-in or zoom transition).
3. Aerial skyline shot right after the hook, before the house.
4. Room-by-room, one selling point per room, no lingering: porch piece-to-camera, kitchen, open living/dining, bedrooms, backyard + deck + fire pit.
5. AI home transformation clip — house turns/rotates while its look changes (Runway, Kling, Higgsfield, Luma). The "wow" beat.
6. Close seated inside with the investment line ("If location is everything in real estate, this one is a smart investment") + comment-word CTA ("Comment WILMORE for more info").

**Copy-paste prompt:** Edit this listing tour in the Kelsie Wilmore format: lifestyle hook in the neighbourhood first, then one unbroken fly-through push-in zoom from the exterior into the interior, an aerial skyline shot, room by room with one selling point each, an AI home-transformation clip where the house turns and changes look, and close seated inside with the investment line plus a comment-word call to action.

## STYLE 6 — Romina's 5 Easy Edits

Creator: Romina Aminian (@romina.aminian). Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfyeGBQzjfm/ (43 s, Nov 15, 2026). Companion guide: https://valley-saxophone-02f.notion.site/The-Reels-Editing-Style-Swipe-File-417f701fb114819f8512ef7947d0206e?pvs=149

Use one or two per video, not all five at full intensity:
1. **Font pairing & placement** — bold display font for key words + clean readable font for explanations; place text like a graphic (beside the face, near the object, in negative space); keep it out of Instagram UI zones.
2. **Blank colour screen** — a full-screen solid colour with one short line between talking-head shots as a visual reset.
3. **Music that matches the energy.**
4. **Sound effects, sparingly** — transitions, text reveals, key movements.
5. **Simple zooms & animations.**

**Copy-paste prompt:** Apply Romina's 5 easy edits to this reel: pair two complementary fonts and treat text like a graphic, insert a blank colour screen between clips as a visual reset, match the music to the video's energy, add sound effects on transitions and key movements sparingly, and layer simple zooms and text animations.

## Workflow — every time raw footage is posted

1. Transcribe and split into beats — one idea per beat (Sumit) or one numbered tip per beat (Kelsie listicle).
2. Pick the style (named by the user, or: explainer = Sumit; tips = Kelsie listicle; house tour = Kelsie listing tour / fly-through; redevelopment = Melissa; shop/showroom = Ariya; quick polish = Romina; motion graphics = MotionSmith SaaS Launch or Vox; paper collage = InVideo paper-cutout; deep hand-built edit = Overstimulated Mind).
3. Sumit: pick the theme colour from mood and topic. Kelsie: set the countdown timer and numbered badges. Ariya: plan the satellite zoom and department order.
4. Storyboard every beat (keyword text, icon/illustration or number badge, animation) and show it before rendering.
5. Build in After Effects via Claude Code + After Effects MCP (AE GPT / Higgsfield where set up), MotionSmith for motion-graphics passes, HyperFrames/Remotion + ffmpeg to render. Sumit follows the 6-step process. If the After Effects MCP is not connected, say which one to connect instead of guessing or silently switching tools.
6. Render vertical 9:16 with subtle sound design. Kelsie: also write the full numbered-list caption.
7. Review checklist: every beat visualized? speaker visible throughout? one theme only (Sumit) / timer and numbers on every tip (Kelsie)? keywords spelled right? pacing tight, no dead air? end card with save + contact CTA (Kelsie)?

## Rules

- Only visualize what was actually said. Never invent quotes, facts or claims.
- Never leave a plain talking-head stretch longer than a few seconds without a visual.
- Keep the speaker's framing clean — graphics sit around them, never covering the face.
- Ask a question only if the footage has no usable audio or the topic is unclear; otherwise proceed and deliver.
- Say which tool was used for the build (After Effects MCP, MotionSmith, or other).

**Input each time:** raw footage, topic, style, target duration, platform (Reels / TikTok / Shorts).
**Output:** the edited video plus a one-line summary (style, theme colour or tip count, beat list); for Kelsie, also the numbered-list caption.
