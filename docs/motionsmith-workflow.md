# MotionSmith-inspired product video workflow

This independent workflow distills the user's supplied livestream notes; it is not a copy of the paid MotionSmith skill, its engines or sound library. The source notes describe a work in progress and do not show the final v2 output. The public [MotionSmith styles page](https://motionsmith.miftahul.com/styles) describes SaaS Launch and Vox Explainer; access to the paid product, its assets and MCP is not established here. Product claims and subscription terms can change; verify them when needed.

## SaaS launch

Use the creator-video route for a product launch or SaaS promo. Analyze the user's reference video and product recordings: pacing, camera, transitions, typography, interface behavior and sound. Use a suitable authorized download or supplied file; reference footage is not automatically licensed for reuse. Record real product facts, approved visuals and permissions. Crop or redact names, emails, tabs and private information before sharing a preview. Do not invent performance claims, prices, testimonials or product features.

Plan a sequence of shots with purpose, visual action, narration, source, timing and transitions. Match length to the story. Choose a restrained brand palette, readable type, editable UI elements and motivated camera movement. Use pixel reveals, counters, 3D card depth, UI selection or scan effects only when they clarify the brief and can actually be built with the chosen tool.

Collect missing assets in a clearly labeled list. Use the user's recordings, appropriately licensed fonts and media, or original artwork. Do not copy paid MotionSmith or Botanica libraries. A screen recording can provide real UI evidence; redraw labels only when they remain faithful to what the user authorized and identify illustrative mockups as such.

Build a motion-first version when requested. Verify real voice timings against the audio before aligning words and actions. Generate or source only authorized sound, then place cues at visible events and mix under speech. Listen to the result and measure loudness where tools permit; figures in the livestream are examples, not mandatory delivery targets. Keep distinct versions and frame-specific notes. Check aspect ratio, text legibility, timing, audio, privacy and actual playback before delivery.

Keep `plan/storyboard.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, `TASTE.md`, an asset manifest, review notes and versioned preview/final outputs inside the new project if the chosen workflow supports them. Record decisions actually approved, unresolved issues, tool and asset provenance, and next steps so a later session can continue. Preserve source files and earlier renders. Follow any specific approval requested by the user, including the existing pop-sticker frame approval.

## Scope and evidence

The supplied notes refer to a live product demonstration, not the full proprietary skill or an independently verified final video. Mentions of specific models, GPU choices, timing, sound levels, services and prices describe that example and should not be treated as requirements for every project. The local style studio generates a brief; it does not render a film or connect to MotionSmith or ElevenLabs.
