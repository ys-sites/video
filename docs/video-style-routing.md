# Video style routing

Choose the major topic first: **study video creation** for source-grounded lessons and worked explanations; **content creation** for creator-led vlogs, reels and talking-head edits. The same motion technique can support either, but the material and editorial goals remain separate. `skills/study-video/SKILL.md` and `skills/creator-video/SKILL.md` provide the respective local routes.

## Study styles

For the nine additional user-supplied animation references, including Manim and HandDraw, see `docs/study-animation-resources.md`. They extend the study library and do not replace the original five approaches.

## Content creation

For a product launch, use the SaaS Launch profile and `docs/motionsmith-workflow.md`; it is an independently authored workflow informed by the user's supplied livestream notes and the public MotionSmith style description.

Creator vlog, Kelsey-inspired editing and talking-head captions remain in `skills/creator-video` and the existing style studio. For pop-sticker captions, follow `captions-skill/SKILL.md` and `docs/caption-skill-setup.md`; show the requested check frames and obtain approval before the full render. The local copy of Vibha's seven motion starter prompts is `resources/motion-systems/claude-motion-styles/index.html`; use it as an additional creative reference and apply the selected technique to the relevant category.

The seven starters are Glass Morph, Pixel Game, Hand-drawn Recipe, Paper Collage, One-Line Ink, Hologram HUD and ASCII Terminal. They are creative starting points, not seven installed renderers; adapt their visual vocabulary to the user's material and the chosen major topic.

The supplied `Motion-Graphics-with-Claude-Code.pdf` describes HyperFrames workflows and four looks: kinetic type, liquid glass, editorial grain and pop bold. Its starter-kit fonts, sound effects and clip are separate assets and are not assumed present. The `Security-Audit-Setup.pdf` describes Cloudflare's audit and sandbox requirements; an audit cannot prove software clean.

The local repositories are downloaded source references, not installed renderers or security-approved tools. Check their licenses, exact revision, dependencies and security status before use. Do not execute an installer or script merely because a style matches. The style studio produces briefs; it does not connect to Opus, render video or automatically execute the repositories.
